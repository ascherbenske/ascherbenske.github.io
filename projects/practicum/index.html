Delay Prediction - Georgia Tech Analytics Practicum

2025 · machine learning (ML) · XGBoost · ML validation · ML evaluation

This is my contribution to my group's project for a client through GT's practicum class. In it, we used ML methods predict the delay of various milestones within a project. The primary goal of the project was to demonstrate to the client the ways in which ML methods could fit into their workflow since, while still based on the client's data, our data was synthetically generated.

My contribution involved the following:

  • Client requirementes development through interviews
  • Exploratory analysis of the data and entity relationships
  • ML model development, tuning, evaluation, and validation
  • Reporting and analysis of results
